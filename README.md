# TerraPlot LR

A free, offline GPS land survey app for Android, designed for Liberia.

- Measure land area and perimeter by walking the corners, walking the boundary, or tapping a map
- Record GPS points with averaged readings for better accuracy
- Convert between latitude/longitude and UTM Zone 29N (WGS84)
- Field sheet with corner coordinates, side lengths and bearings
- Areas in m², hectares, acres, square feet and Liberian lots

Phone GPS is usually accurate to about 3–5 m in open sky. Results are for
planning, checking and recording; legal boundary surveys need a licensed surveyor.
